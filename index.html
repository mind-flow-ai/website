Why Physics-Based

The forecasting problem traders actually face

Locational marginal prices are determined by Security-Constrained Economic Dispatch — a Linear Program that minimizes generation cost subject to power balance, transmission limits, and ancillary service requirements. The LP's dual variables become LMPs. This mathematical structure has properties that make pure statistical prediction systematically hard.

LMPs change discontinuously when binding constraints change. On a normal day, transmission limits are slack and prices reflect the marginal generator. On a stressed day, specific constraints bind and prices jump. The transition is structural, not gradual. State-of-the-art ML — even Temporal Fusion Transformers — fits the smooth regime well but breaks on the regime transitions that produce the largest commercial value.

Sophisticated traders also have private information that no third-party model captures: specific generator outages, weather forecasts they trust more than defaults, fuel constraints, transmission maintenance schedules. They cannot use this information effectively with statistical tools because those tools produce outputs the trader cannot configure with their own knowledge.

MindFlow.ai builds the simulator they need. A Linear Programming replica of dispatch combined with empirical models of participant behavior, exposed as configurable forecasting infrastructure. Defaults from validated behavioral models for inputs the trader doesn't override; the LP solves with whatever combination of defaults and overrides is specified. The trader stops trying to learn the LP from data and starts solving it directly with the right inputs.

Technology

Physics + empirical, exposed as infrastructure

The simulator is a composition of three layers, each chosen because the market demands it. Linear programming reproduces the optimization that actually clears the market every five minutes. Empirical behavioral models fill in the inputs the LP needs from the real world. A topology-independent core means the same engine adapts to ERCOT, PJM, and CAISO through parser adapters, not rewrites.

This architecture is what makes it possible to use AI in a mission-critical physics environment without hallucinating power flows — and to ship it as configurable infrastructure rather than a black-box prediction service.

⚗

Linear Programming Dispatch

An LP replica of Security-Constrained Economic Dispatch with continuous variables for generator dispatch, an objective minimizing dispatch cost, and constraints including power balance at each bus plus line flow limits enforced through the physics engine. The same output structure ERCOT's SCED produces — LMPs, flows, binding constraint shadow prices — but with every input configurable by the client. Solver-agnostic: HiGHS, Gurobi, or CPLEX.

🧠

Empirical Behavioral Models

Per-fuel XGBoost commitment predictors trained on ERCOT historical data, validated at 3.14× over the day-ahead market on CCGT90. Battery fleet dispatch using persistence-D1/D7 mean with recency-based conformal calibration, selected as the empirical winner over five XGBoost variants. The methodology that empirically wins for each prediction target, not uniform sophistication.

🌐

Topology-Independent Physics Engine

DC-OPF with PTDF/LODF computation, lazy N-1 contingency enforcement, piecewise-linear cost curves, and generic constraint structures (ERCOT GTCs, PJM flowgates, CAISO paths). The engine operates on a canonical internal representation of buses, branches, generators, and constraints — ERCOT's FNM, PJM's network data, and academic test cases all plug into the same core through their respective parsers. Validated on MATPOWER case9, ACTIVSg500, and ACTIVSg2000 ahead of FNM access.

⚙

Override-First Configuration

Clients override any input through the configuration interface: generator outage status, custom wind/solar forecasts, fleet commitment overrides, transmission derates, contingency scenarios. Each override flows through to the LP as input; the simulator solves with whatever combination of empirical defaults and trader-injected information was specified. Validation logic catches inconsistent overrides before they reach the solver.

Why this architecture: The most commercially valuable LMP forecasts — what happens during stress events when transmission constraints bind unexpectedly — are exactly where pure ML systematically fails, because the underlying price formation is a Linear Program with discontinuous regime transitions. Pure physics requires every input specified manually, which is impractical for daily operations. MindFlow.ai's hybrid is engineered for the gap: empirical behavioral models for inputs the trader doesn't override, LP-based solving once inputs are specified. Higher-fidelity capabilities — AC power flow, SCUC replication, GNN-accelerated scenario search, narrow-scope reinforcement learning — are deferred to V3, gated on V2 commercial traction revealing which advancements have the clearest client value.
